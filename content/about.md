### Who I Am
I'm a software engineer who thrives at the intersection of application development, distributed systems, and infrastructure.

### My Approach
Code is just a tool; reliable systems are the goal. I believe the best engineers are pragmatic problem-solvers who understand that every architectural decision has a cost, and that "boring" technology often makes for the best sleep schedules.

### Why I Write
I created this site to document my journey, share the messy reality of building software at scale, and connect with other engineers who care deeply about the craft.
